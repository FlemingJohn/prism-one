Choice

Categorizes state into a closed set of options (e.g. Engineering SOP, Legal Policy, Billing Invoice). Supplies calibrated probabilities for every single choice.

Noul

A probabilistic boolean. Evaluates a semantic proposition and returns an epistemically honest float between 0.0 and 1.0 representing certainty.
